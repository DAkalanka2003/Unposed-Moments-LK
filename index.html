Who We Are

Unposed Moments began with one idea: the best photograph of your day is the one you didn't know was being taken.

We're a small Kiribathgoda-based crew of photographers who'd rather melt into the background of your celebration than direct it. No stiff line-ups, no "look here and smile" — just quiet, attentive cameras, ready the instant something real happens between you and the people you love.

Weddings, pre-shoots, graduations and everything in between — every gallery we deliver reads like a story you lived, not one we staged.
